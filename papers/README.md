# Downloaded Papers

## Evasion Methods

1. **SICO: Large Language Models can be Guided to Evade AI-Generated Text Detection** (2305.10847_SICO_guided_evasion.pdf)
   - Authors: Lu et al.
   - Year: 2024 (TMLR)
   - arXiv: 2305.10847
   - Why relevant: Iterative prompt optimization against proxy detector; closest existing work to our hypothesis

2. **CoPA: Contrastive Paraphrase Attacks on LLM-Generated Text Detectors** (2505.15337_CoPA_contrastive_paraphrase.pdf)
   - Authors: Fang et al.
   - Year: 2025 (EMNLP)
   - arXiv: 2505.15337
   - Why relevant: Training-free contrastive decoding; token-level iterative self-correction

3. **Adversarial Paraphrasing: A Universal Attack for Humanizing AI-Generated Text** (2506.07001_adversarial_paraphrasing.pdf)
   - Authors: Cheng et al.
   - Year: 2025 (NeurIPS)
   - arXiv: 2506.07001
   - Why relevant: Detector-guided token selection; universal transferability across detectors

4. **Self-Disguise Attack** (2508.15848_self_disguise_attack.pdf)
   - Authors: Zhou et al.
   - Year: 2025
   - arXiv: 2508.15848
   - Why relevant: Explicit iterative rejection loop for feature extraction; RAG-based example retrieval

5. **Humanizing Machine-Generated Content (HMGC)** (2404.01907_humanizing_machine_content.pdf)
   - Authors: Zhou, He, Sun
   - Year: 2024
   - arXiv: 2404.01907
   - Why relevant: Word-level adversarial substitution with iterative refinement until threshold met

6. **Paraphrasing Evades Detectors of AI-Generated Text (DIPPER)** (2303.13408_paraphrasing_evades_detectors.pdf)
   - Authors: Krishna et al.
   - Year: 2023 (NeurIPS)
   - arXiv: 2303.13408
   - Why relevant: Foundational paraphrase evasion work; standard baseline
